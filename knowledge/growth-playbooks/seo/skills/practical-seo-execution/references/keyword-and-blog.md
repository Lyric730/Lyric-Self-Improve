# Keyword And Blog Reference

Use this reference for keyword research, topic selection, blog briefs, and SEO/AEO writing.

## Keyword Inputs

Build seed terms from:

- Product functions: use searchable function names, not internal feature nicknames.
- ICP pain: use the words users use when stuck.
- Competitor and alternative intent: `best`, `tools`, `alternative`, `vs`, `compare`.
- Emerging ecosystem terms: AI search, answer engines, new standards, regulatory shifts, or platform changes.

Avoid pure model-generated expansion as the only source. Treat it as brainstorming, not evidence.

## Expansion Sources

Prioritize:

1. Keyword tools with historical metrics, such as Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush, KWFinder, DataForSEO.
2. Real community questions from Reddit, Quora, HN, Indie Hackers, forums, comments, support threads, and reviews.
3. Google PAA, related searches, autocomplete, and SERP snippets.
4. Competitor pages and comparison SERPs.

Community filters:

- Prefer question posts.
- Avoid show-off, launch, and announcement posts as demand evidence.
- Use score, comment count, repeated questions, and recency as evidence strength.
- Do not overvalue a single isolated post.

## Validation Signals

Check:

- SERP Top 10 strength and content type.
- Search intent.
- Search volume, trend, and KD when available.
- Community evidence.
- PAA and related-search depth.
- AI Overview or answer-engine citation opportunity.
- ICP match.
- Product fit.

Stop validating when more research will not change the next action.

## Scoring

Use a simple weighted table:

| Dimension | What To Judge |
|---|---|
| Volume | Whether there is search demand |
| KD | Whether a young site can compete |
| Intent | Whether the intent matches useful content or commercial discovery |
| Growth | Whether the topic is rising |
| ICP | Whether it matches the target user |
| Product fit | Whether the product can naturally help |

Tiering:

- `Tier 1`: produce soon.
- `Tier 1.5`: low-volume but strong ICP/product/community fit.
- `Tier 2`: useful but lower priority.
- `Tier 3`: keep as reserve.
- `Negative`: exclude.

## Pillar And Cluster

Prefer cluster-first execution for new sites. Clusters target concrete problems and often have weaker competition.

Pillar criteria:

- Information-oriented topic.
- At least five credible clusters can sit under it.
- At least one cluster has strong data or community evidence.
- The product or team can add a differentiated view.

Cluster criteria:

- Solves one specific problem.
- SERP competition is not overwhelming.
- Has community, PAA, or tool evidence.
- Can connect naturally to product capability.

## Blog Brief Requirements

Do not draft before the brief is complete.

Every brief should include:

- Primary keyword.
- 5-8 secondary keywords.
- 5-8 LSI or related expressions.
- Search intent.
- ICP and pain.
- Article type.
- SERP common answers.
- Differentiated angle.
- Evidence pool with source URLs.
- H1, TL;DR, H2/H3, FAQ.
- Internal links.
- Product link.
- Authority external links.
- Title, meta description, slug, tags, and schema plan.

## Article Type Selection

| Intent | Use |
|---|---|
| User wants tools | List post or buyer guide |
| User wants steps | Step-by-step |
| User wants a concept | Expanded definition or beginner guide |
| User compares options | Comparison |
| SERP is generic | Contrarian or evidence-heavy article |

## Drafting Rules

- Lead with a direct answer.
- Include a TL;DR when the query benefits from answer extraction.
- Make H2s match real user questions where possible.
- Give every H2 evidence, a judgment, or a concrete action.
- Use tables, lists, examples, and decision rules instead of long undifferentiated paragraphs.
- Make important chunks self-contained so answer engines can quote them.
- Use current sources when the topic changes quickly.
- Avoid stuffing keywords or forcing product mentions.

## Linking Rules

Each article should include:

- Same-topic internal links.
- At least one relevant product or conversion link.
- Authority external links to original or high-trust sources.

Do not invent URLs. Verify links before publishing.

Use varied anchor text:

- Exact match.
- Partial match.
- Brand or URL.
- Generic anchor when natural.

## Publish Checks

Verify:

- Title and meta description are concise and specific.
- Primary keyword appears in title, H1, opening, slug, meta, and at least one H2 when natural.
- Frontmatter or CMS fields are complete.
- Images have meaningful alt text unless decorative.
- Internal links resolve.
- External links resolve.
- Schema matches the article type.
- The article answers the query without forcing a sales page tone.
