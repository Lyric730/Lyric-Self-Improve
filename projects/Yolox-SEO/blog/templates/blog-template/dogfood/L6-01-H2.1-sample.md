## Do you actually need AEO services right now?

**Most SMB brands don't — yet.** Answer engine optimization services make sense
when your content is already ranking but not getting cited by AI surfaces. If
your blog has fewer than 20 pages or zero organic traffic, AEO services will
spend $2k-5k/month optimizing pages nobody reads. Fix the organic baseline first,
then layer AEO on top.

The clearest signal you're ready: at least one page on your site appeared in
Google's AI Overview or Perplexity in the last 30 days — even briefly. That
means AI engines already know you exist; the work now is to get cited more
often and on higher-intent queries. Without that baseline, AEO services are
optimizing into a vacuum.

### When you need AEO services

- **You have 30+ published pages** with at least 5 ranking on page 1 of Google
- **AI Overview now appears** on 20%+ of your target keywords (check manually
  or with [AlsoAsked](https://alsoasked.com))
- **Revenue depends on inbound** — you can directly tie $X per AI-cited page
- **You don't have 5-10 h/week** to learn AEO and rewrite chunks yourself
- **Your team lacks schema / structured data expertise** in-house

If 3 or more of these apply, AEO services pay back inside 6 months at most
budget tiers we benchmarked.

### When you don't (yet)

- **Fewer than 20 indexed pages** — fix content volume first
- **AI Overview never appears** on your target keywords — your category isn't
  AI-surface-ready yet (B2B niche, regulated industries lag here)
- **Solo founder under $50k MRR** — DIY path is cheaper and faster to learn
- **You haven't shipped schema markup** at all yet — that's a $0 fix you can
  do this weekend, not a $3k/month retainer

For brands in the "not yet" bucket, the right next step is a one-time audit
($500-1.5k) — not a monthly retainer. Audit tells you what to fix; you fix it
yourself; revisit retainer decision in 6 months.

### A quick gut check

| Signal | Ready for AEO services | Skip for now |
|---|---|---|
| Published pages | 30+ | <20 |
| AI Overview appearances | 20%+ of target keywords | 0% |
| In-house schema skill | None | Have one engineer |
| Budget | $2k+/month | <$500/month |
| Time available | <5 h/week | 10+ h/week |

If you land mostly on the right column, our recommendation: skip retainer.
Run a one-time schema audit (try [Sophie](https://yolox.ai/agents-store/sophie),
our free AEO Doctor agent, for the audit itself), ship the fixes yourself, and
revisit the buying decision after you have 30+ pages.

If you land mostly on the left column, continue to the evaluation criteria
below — the 7 vendors we reviewed all fit somewhere on that spectrum.
