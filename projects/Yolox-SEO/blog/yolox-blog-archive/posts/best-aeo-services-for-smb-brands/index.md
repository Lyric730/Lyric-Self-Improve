## Do you actually need answer engine optimization services right now?

**Most SMB brands don't — yet.** AEO services make sense when your content is already ranking but failing to get cited by AI surfaces. If your blog has fewer than 20 indexed pages or zero organic traffic, AEO services will spend $2k-$5k/month optimizing pages nobody reads. Fix the organic baseline first, then layer AEO on top.

The clearest readiness signal: at least one page on your site appeared in Google's AI Overview, Perplexity, or ChatGPT in the last 30 days — even briefly. That means AI engines already know you exist; the remaining work is to get cited more often and on higher-intent queries. Without that baseline, AEO services are optimizing into a vacuum.

### When you need AEO services

- **You have 30+ published pages** with at least 5 ranking on page 1 of Google
- **AI Overview appears** on 20%+ of your target keywords
- **Revenue depends on inbound** — you can tie real $ per AI-cited page
- **You lack 5-10 hours per week** to learn AEO and rewrite chunks in-house
- **Your team has no schema or structured data expertise**

If 3 or more apply, AEO services typically pay back inside 6 months at most retainer tiers.

### When you don't (yet)

- **Fewer than 20 indexed pages** — fix content volume first
- **AI Overview never appears** on your category — your niche isn't AI-surface-ready yet
- **Solo founder under $50k MRR** — DIY path is cheaper and faster to learn
- **You haven't shipped any schema markup** — that's a weekend fix, not a $3k/month retainer

For brands in the "not yet" bucket, the right next step is a one-time audit ($1.5k-$5k), not a monthly retainer. ## How to evaluate AEO services (7 criteria most buyers miss)

Vendor quality varies far more than pricing. The 7 criteria below separate substance from "tool-resold visibility tracking and quarterly content brief" packages ([Digital Strategy Force, 2026](https://digitalstrategyforce.com/journal/how-much-should-an-aeo-agency-cost-in-2026/)).

**1. Schema coverage breadth**
The vendor should handle FAQPage, HowTo, Article, Organization, and Product schema across your full site — not just one or two types. **Deal-breaker**: only Article schema deployment.

**2. AI citation tracking (not just rankings)**
Do they measure Google AI Overview, Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Claude citations — or just SERP positions? **Deal-breaker**: no AI surface tracking. You can't optimize what you don't measure.

**3. Chunk-level content restructuring**
As Aleyda Solis ([Orainti](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/aeo-vs-geo)) put it: *"With AI search this happens at a passage or chunk level of relevance."* The vendor must rewrite content into self-contained 50-150 word chunks that LLMs can extract cleanly. **Deal-breaker**: vendor talks about "long-form authority content" without chunk strategy.

**4. Per-page output cadence**
4 pages/month minimum for SMB engagement. Lower = retainer doesn't compound. **Nice-to-have**: 8+ pages/month or 1 full silo per quarter.

**5. Pricing transparency**
Real numbers on the website versus "Contact sales for pricing." Hidden tool markup is rampant — agencies bundle platform licenses with **30-50% undisclosed markup** ([Digital Strategy Force, May 2026](https://digitalstrategyforce.com/journal/how-much-should-an-aeo-agency-cost-in-2026/)). **Deal-breaker**: no public pricing tier on website.

**6. Vendor's own AEO performance**
If they can't get themselves cited in AI Overviews for "AEO services" or "answer engine optimization," they can't help you either. Search both phrases — vendor should appear in AI Overview top 3 sources.

**7. Contract terms**
Month-to-month is healthy. **Deal-breaker**: 12-month minimum with no opt-out. AEO is too new an industry to lock in for a year.

## 4 red flags that mean walk away

**Red flag 1: "We guarantee top citation in ChatGPT"**
No one can guarantee this. ChatGPT's citation logic is opaque, non-deterministic, and changes with each model update. A vendor making this promise is either lying or doesn't understand the technology.

**Red flag 2: All-inclusive $99/month "AEO package"**
The math doesn't work. Genuine schema audit + content restructuring + citation tracking takes 5-15 hours per month minimum. $99/month means the work is template-only or outsourced offshore — likely both.

**Red flag 3: No monthly citation report**
If you can't see month-over-month change in AI citations, you have no way to measure ROI. [GrackerAI's 2026 cybersecurity AEO benchmark](https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/73-cybersecurity-vendors-are-invisible-chatgpt-grackerai-benchmark-finds-ai) found **73% of cybersecurity vendors received zero ChatGPT citations** in their own category (100 companies across 6 AI platforms × 250 prompts) — a directional signal for any B2B vertical. Without reporting, you're flying blind.

**Red flag 4: Refuses to run a baseline citation audit**
If a vendor won't measure your starting point, they're protecting themselves from being judged on real outcomes. Insist on a 30-day baseline before committing to any retainer.

## FAQ

### How much do AEO services cost in 2026?

AEO services range from **$1.5k for a one-time audit to $60k+/month for embedded enterprise retainers**. Most SMB-fit packages sit at $2k-$8k/month for starter or strategic retainers, with content production and schema management included. yolox offers a free + credit-based alternative for brands willing to self-serve.

### Can I do AEO myself?

Yes — schema deployment, llms.txt creation, and chunk-level content rewriting are all doable in-house if you have 5-10 hours per week and basic technical comfort. The yolox AI agent stack automates most of the repetitive work for free. Most SMBs under $50k MRR should DIY for 6 months before considering a retainer.

### What's the difference between SEO and AEO services?

**SEO services** optimize for Google organic ranking — keywords, backlinks, traditional on-page factors. **AEO services** optimize for citation inside AI-generated answers (Google AI Overview, Perplexity, ChatGPT, Claude). The work overlaps on schema and content quality but diverges on chunk-level structure, llms.txt, and citation tracking. Most modern agencies sell both, but few specialize in AEO with measurable citation outcomes. For a deeper comparison see our [AEO vs GEO breakdown](https://yolox.ai/blog/best-aeo-services-for-smb-brands).

### How long does AEO take to show results?

Typically **8-12 weeks for first AI Overview appearances** on low-difficulty terms (KD <20), and **3-6 months for competitive terms** (KD 30+). Pillar topics and brand searches can take 6-12 months. If a vendor promises results in under 30 days, they're likely measuring SERP impressions instead of citations.

### Do I need both SEO and AEO services?

For most SMB content brands in 2026, **yes**. Organic Google still drives 60%+ of search traffic, but AI Overview already takes 25% of SERP real estate and that share grows monthly. Pure SEO without AEO leaves citation opportunity on the table; pure AEO without SEO baseline has no foundation to optimize from. The right answer depends on your traffic mix — check GA4 to see your AI-referred sessions before deciding which to prioritize.
